EU AI Act: A Formal Taxonomy

The Act partitions every system into four mutually exclusive tiers.

The four tiers

  • Unacceptable — prohibited outright (social scoring, manipulation)
  • High — allowed with strict duties (hiring, credit, medical)
  • Limited — transparency obligation only (disclose it is AI)
  • Minimal — no special rules (most everyday tools)

Tier determines duty. Classification is the whole legal mechanism.

European Union, "The EU Artificial Intelligence Act", 2024.

The NIST RMF as a Loop

Four functions run continuously: Govern wraps Map, Measure, Manage.

A cycle, not a checklist: monitor after release, then map again.

NIST, "AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)", 2023.

What "Measurable" Looks Like

The Measure function needs numbers defined in earlier lectures.

Attack-success rate

Fraction of jailbreak or attack attempts that get through.

Coverage

Share of watermarked or detectable content correctly flagged.

Calibration error

Gap between a model's stated confidence and its real accuracy.

Each was defined in its own lecture; governance just reuses them as audit metrics.

## E03 — fairness criteria and postprocessing [published result]
Hardt, Price, Srebro, NeurIPS 2016, arXiv v1, https://arxiv.org/html/1610.02413v1 . Sections 2, 4; Definitions 2.1–2.2 and 4.1; Figures 1–2. Equalized odds matches conditional decision rates for both recorded classes; opportunity matches the positive-class rate only. Postprocessing uses group plus score/prediction; held-out labels are needed to estimate performance. A common attainable ROC point may require randomized thresholds; the overlap of attainable regions, not arbitrary points below an imagined curve, is the relevant set. This does not establish that a selected criterion is socially sufficient or that labels are unbiased.

## E05 — predictive parity, unequal errors, COMPAS plots [published result]
Chouldechova, Big Data 2017, arXiv v1 https://arxiv.org/html/1703.00056v1 , §1.2, §2.1–2.3, Eq. (2.6), Figs. 1–3. Identity FPR = p/(1-p) × (1-PPV)/PPV × (1-FNR). With 0<p<1, 0<PPV<1 and FNR<1, equal PPV plus equal FNR plus equal FPR imply equal base rates. Do not say calibration alone implies unequal error rates, or that every pair conflicts. Fig. 1 assesses outcome rates by decile and PPV above cutoffs: a COMPAS decile is not a probability. Fig. 2(b) is misdemeanor defendants by prior-count bin, with uncertainty, not a causal adjustment experiment. Retrieved full text and identity.

## E08 — reweighing [published result + our calculation]
Kamiran and Calders, KAIS 33 (2012), §5.2, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10115-011-0463-8 . Weight = independence-expected cell share / observed cell share. The weighted empirical group/label table becomes independent when every required cell has support. Baseline toy: group s (+20,−30), group o (+40,−10); weights (1.5, 2/3, 3/4, 2); weighted cells (30,20,30,20), total 100, positive share 60% in each group. This changes weights, not people's outcomes/labels, and does not guarantee test-prediction parity. Arithmetic independently checked for this plan; publisher metadata/abstract retrieved, baseline notes supply exact §5.2 provenance.

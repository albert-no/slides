## Common note specifications (all new/changed articles) [judgment]
1. **Must:** lead with the actual point in one or two short, plain sentences; not a title repeat, generic topic label or speaking instruction. Keep the qualifier necessary for truth. Then explain how to read this particular exhibit. Details and source references follow. A divider/title/closer can have one useful sentence; do not pad it into a rigid template.
2. **Must:** plot reading identifies setup/population, axes and units, encodings/panels, direction where relevant, observed comparison and scope. Table reading identifies the denominator and meaningful row/column comparison. Diagram reading follows objects/arrows/actions. Equations explain symbols, intuition and assumptions; examples distinguish schematic from measured. Not every plot detail belongs in the opening two sentences.
3. **Must:** reconcile the **whole affected article**, including retained scripts, limitations and citation captions. Mechanical wrapping of contradictory old prose does not count. Preserve distinct proofs, minor details, references, timing cues and uncertainty; remove duplicates only with a disposition. No whole-entry word limit on reference detail.
4. **Must:** directly relevant source with version/date and precise figure/table/section where available. Historical claims stay dated; no silent leaderboard/model/current-status refresh. Verify a newly asserted fact or changed interpretation from its primary source. A baseline takeaway is intent, not evidence. Record new source support in the work report for owner addition to evidence.md.
5. **Must:** one article per slide, matching ID/title/order; changes on a slide and its note land together. Required qualifiers/answers remain on the main slide; longer explanations live in the note. Preserve established technical detail rather than forcing it onto main slides.
6. **Prefer:** short readable prose and restrained hierarchy, English retained. Headings/wording and presentation are slides' contextual judgment. No compulsory extra Key-takeaway line if it repeats the opening. No prescribed markup, CSS, pixel values, font sizes or rigid new visual template.
7. **Acceptance:** a lecturer reading the opening can say the message; reading the next explanation can explain the visible exhibit; details support both. All source/assumption layers agree. No missing material, broken links/math, unreadable wraps or new horizontal overflow. Keep default type; split/reflow content instead of shrinking it.

Which Line of the Recipe Changed

  1. Examples. No answer key. Just the data.
  2. Family. Unchanged. A stack of boxes.
  3. Loss. New: score a guess the data can check itself.
  4. Search. Unchanged. Feel the slope, step, repeat.

One line changed. That line unlocked the internet as training data.

Why This Is Not an Ordinary Bug

Ordinary softwareAn agent
Does the wrong thing onceKeeps trying until something works
Fails where you deployed itEnds up somewhere you never deployed it
Fixed by patching the holeFinds the next hole after the patch

The difference is persistence. Plan for something that keeps going.

What to Ask on Monday

  1. Reach. What can this agent touch, and what can it never touch?
  2. Keys. Which credentials does it hold, and who revokes them?
  3. Log. Would we notice on day one, or on day three?
  4. Gate. Which actions require a person to approve, always?

Trustworthy AI is mostly boring plumbing, decided before deployment.

Today in Five Lines

  • A model is a function. Nothing more.
  • Training is a search: examples, family, loss, slope.
  • Drop the labels and the internet becomes your training set.
  • Reverse the arrow and the same recipe creates.
  • Where a machine can mark the work, progress is fast, and cheap.

Everything in this course was one recipe, pointed at harder things.

Powerful.

Not yet trustworthy.

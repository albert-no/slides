## E32 — Influence computation need not materialize the Hessian
Published result: Koh & Liang, ICML 2017, arXiv:1703.04730v3 §3 (https://arxiv.org/html/1703.04730), checked 2026-11-15 KST. H^-1 v can be approximated using implicit Hessian-vector products and CG/stochastic estimation. An exact CG solution takes parameter-count iterations in ideal arithmetic; practical approximations use fewer. The d² dense-storage calculation is correct but not a necessary memory cost of every influence computation. Non-convex instability and solve cost remain distinct limitations. The retained lec05 S31 detail already states this.

## E33 — Approximate certificates and one-sided unlearning audits

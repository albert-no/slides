What Safety Training Adds

A chat model learns to refuse some requests. What would it mean to get around that, and how would we count it?

Chat Assistants Help, but Decline Some Requests

Declining is a safeguard added by training, on top of a capable model.

Jailbreak: an input that gets the answer anyway.

Why Getting Around Refusal Matters

A bypass can turn a refusal into real-world help or a real action.

Today: how robust is refusal, and how do we measure it?

Scaling the Attack Budget

What happens when the attacker simply tries many times, or packs in many examples?

Test-Time Scaling: Spend More Compute per Question

Same weights: only the compute per question grows.

Snell et al., "Scaling LLM Test-Time Compute Optimally can be More Effective than Scaling Model Parameters", arXiv 2408.03314, 2024 — Section 2 and Fig. 5 · Brown et al., "Large Language Monkeys", arXiv 2407.21787, 2024 — Fig. 1.

Sampling 250 Times Beat the Best Single Answer

Bar % of SWE-bench Lite issues (real GitHub bugs) solved by at least one sample

Works when answers can be checked automatically, as code can by tests.

Without a checker, majority vote and reward models plateaued after a few hundred samples.

Many Tries Turn a Small Rate Into a Large One

At least one success in $N$ tries: $1-(1-p)^N$, with $p = 1\%$ per try.

Illustrative, not from a study: assumes independent tries with the same $p$.

More examples, lower loss: a power law, even on harmless tasks.

Training Copies a Costly Teacher's Verdicts

Teacher a prompted classifier with every policy document · reasons before it answers

Data per harm type, 1,024 harmful + 1,024 hard-benign scenarios · half non-English

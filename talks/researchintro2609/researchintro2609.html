What a Project Is

Novel, nontrivial, not a copy

The Problem Nobody Hands You

The first box is the hard one.

Project as Artwork

An artist facing a blank canvas has your problem: no assignment, and a judging audience.

The artist asks

  • Has this been made before?
  • Is it more than decoration?
  • Can I say what it does?

You ask

  • Has this been published before?
  • Is it more than a script?
  • Can I say why it matters?

Same three questions, different studio.

What a Project Owes

New

Some claim here did not exist.

Nontrivial

Not one script, not one prompt.

Defensible

You can say why it matters.

The bar is not "a paper". The bar is all three.

Not a Project

What it looks likeWhy it is not a project
Follow a tutorial repositorySomeone else's plan, executed
Reimplement a paper, and stopSkilful, and still a copy
Same method, swapped datasetA known result at a new size
One prompt to Claude or GPTFive minutes, and no claim

All four are good exercises. A project is what you do next.

Where to Start

Reproduce, then find what breaks

Start by Reproducing

Copying a known result is not the project. It is the fastest way into one.

You cannot see a gap in a field you have never run.

What Reproduction Breaks

What you findThe question it opens
Numbers land three points lowWhich unreported choice carried the gain?
Works on their dataset onlyWhich assumption does the next one break?
One hyperparameter dominatesIs the method the result, or the tuning?
The baseline was undertrainedDoes the gain survive a fair baseline?

Right-hand column, one line each. That is a shortlist.

A Reproduction Becomes a Project

  1. Reproduce. A retrieval-augmented QA paper. Their code, one week.
  2. Break. Retrieval recall matches. End-to-end accuracy is six points low.
  3. Locate. Their reranker was tuned on the evaluation queries.
  4. Project. How much reported gain survives an untuned reranker?

The paper handed you a question by being slightly wrong.

Three Ways In

Break it

Run a published result. Report where it stops holding.

Move it

Take a working method somewhere its assumptions fail.

Measure it

Build the evaluation the field keeps arguing without.

All three begin with somebody else's work.

Escalating a Fine-Tune

  1. Task. Fine-tune Llama on hospital QA. One script, one afternoon.
  2. And then? It answers in the right tone. Are the facts new?
  3. Problem. Separate style transfer from knowledge gain.
  4. Question. Does fine-tuning add facts, or only reformat old ones?

Now it is hard: you need facts provably absent from the base model.

Escalating a RAG Bot

  1. Task. RAG over lecture PDFs. One prompt to an agent.
  2. And then? It answers confidently when retrieval missed.
  3. Problem. Predict retrieval failure before answering.
  4. Question. Can the model flag hops that retrieval never returned?

Deliverable: a coverage-versus-accuracy curve, not a demo.

Escalating an RL Agent

  1. Task. Train PPO on a standard environment. A tutorial.
  2. And then? High return, and the behaviour looks wrong.
  3. Problem. Detect reward hacking before deployment.
  4. Question. Does return collapse under held-out respecifications of the same intent?

Nontrivial because you must build the family of respecifications.

How to Survey

  1. One recent survey paper, read fully
  2. Its top-cited references, abstracts only
  3. Last two years of NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL
  4. Three lab pages, and what they stopped doing
  5. Any workshop call: the open problems list

A workshop call for papers is a field's own list of what is unsolved.

Read More Than Feels Reasonable

GPT as Accelerator, Not Author

Ask it to

  • Summarise 20 abstracts
  • Find where two papers disagree
  • Attack your idea as a reviewer

Do not ask it to

  • Give you a research topic
  • Judge whether the idea is new
  • Decide what is interesting

An idea handed over in one turn fails the single-prompt test.

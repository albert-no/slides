01

Gaussian Diffusion Theory

SDEs, Fokker–Planck, time reversal, and the score

Continuous Diffusion at a Glance

Forward SDE corrupts data with noise; learn the time-reversed SDE driven by the score $\nabla_x \log p_t(x)$.

Drives image, video, audio generation: Stable Diffusion, Imagen, DALL·E, Sora.

Song, Sohl-Dickstein, Kingma, Kumar, Ermon, Poole, "Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations", ICLR 2021 Oral.

Forward SDE and Its Marginal Law

Noising as an Itô diffusion (VP: $f=-\tfrac{\beta_t}{2}x$; VE: $f=0$):

$$dX_t = f(X_t,t)\,dt + g(t)\,dW_t,\qquad X_0\sim p_{\text{data}}.$$

The marginal density $p_t$ solves the Fokker–Planck PDE:

$$\partial_t\,p_t(x) = -\partial_x\bigl(f(x,t)\,p_t(x)\bigr) + \tfrac{g^2(t)}{2}\,\partial_x^2\,p_t(x).$$

Training tracks the marginals $\{p_t\}$, never the sample paths.

Reverse SDE and the Score

Anderson's time reversal — same marginals $p_t$, run backward:

$$d\bar X_t = \bigl(f - g^2\,\partial_x\log p_t(\bar X_t)\bigr)\,dt + g(t)\,d\bar W_t.$$

Deterministic probability-flow ODE, identical marginals:

$$\dot{\bar X}_t = f - \tfrac{1}{2}\,g^2\,\partial_x\log p_t(\bar X_t).$$

Everything reduces to the score $\partial_x\log p_t$ — learned by denoising score matching.

Anderson, "Reverse-time diffusion equation models", Stoch. Proc. Appl. 1982; Vincent, Neural Computation 2011.

Masked Diffusion at a Glance

Forward progressively masks tokens; reverse unmasks any-order from bidirectional context.

Discrete Analogue: A Ratio, Not a Gradient

On a finite state space the gradient $\partial_x\log p_t$ has no meaning. Forward and reverse jump rates on a continuous-time Markov chain:

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Forward:}\quad & Q_t(x \to y), \\[2pt] \text{Reverse:}\quad & \bar Q_t(y \to x) \;=\; Q_t(x \to y)\,\cdot\,\frac{p_t(x)}{p_t(y)}. \end{aligned}$$

The reverse process is driven by the ratio $p_t(x)/p_t(y)$ — the discrete analogue of the score.

Discrete Diffusion and Its Losses

Concrete scores, score entropy, and the time-free reduction

SEDD

Concrete Score and the Reverse Rate

Continuous-time Markov chain on a vocabulary, rate matrix $Q_t$:   $\partial_t\,p_t = Q_t\,p_t.$

The reverse chain's rate is built from the concrete score $p_t(y)/p_t(x)$:

$$\bar Q_t(x,y) \;=\; \frac{p_t(y)}{p_t(x)}\,Q_t(y,x),\qquad y\neq x.$$

Learn $s_\theta(x,t)_y \approx \dfrac{p_t(y)}{p_t(x)}$ — a vector of ratios, the discrete score.

Lou, Meng, Ermon, "Discrete Diffusion Modeling by Estimating the Ratios of the Data Distribution", ICML 2024.
SEDD

Score Entropy: A Tractable Loss

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{SE}} \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{t,\,x_0,\,x_t}\!\left[\sum_{y\neq x_t} w_t(x_t,y)\!\left(\,s_\theta(y\mid x_t) \;-\; \frac{p_t(y\mid x_0)}{p_t(x_t\mid x_0)}\,\log s_\theta(y\mid x_t)\,\right)\right]$$
  • A Bregman divergence in $s_\theta$ — uniquely minimized at the true ratio.
  • Intractable marginal ratio swapped for the known conditional kernel — discrete Vincent.

First discrete diffusion to match GPT-2 perplexity.

RADD

Absorbing Score Is Time-Free

Theorem (Ou et al., 2024).

For absorbing (masked) diffusion the concrete score factorizes:

$$s_t(y\mid x_t) \;=\; \underbrace{\frac{1-\alpha_t}{\alpha_t}}_{\text{known scalar}} \cdot\; \underbrace{p_\theta\bigl(y \,\big|\, x_t^{\text{unmasked}}\bigr)}_{\text{time-free clean conditional}}.$$

The clean conditional depends on which tokens are revealed, not on $t$ — so the network drops $t$ as an input.

Ou et al., "Your Absorbing Discrete Diffusion Secretly Models the Conditional Distributions of Clean Data", NeurIPS 2024.
RADD

ELBO $=$ Any-Order Autoregressive NLL

The absorbing NELBO collapses to a reweighted cross-entropy on masked tokens:

$$-\log p_\theta(x_0) \;\le\; \int_0^1 w(t)\,\mathbb{E}_{x_t}\!\left[\sum_{i\in\mathcal{M}_t} -\log p_\theta\bigl(x_0^{(i)} \mid x_t^{\text{unmasked}}\bigr)\right]dt,\quad w(t)=\tfrac{-\alpha_t'}{1-\alpha_t}.$$

Equivalently, an expectation over a uniform random order $\sigma$:

$$\mathbb{E}_{\sigma}\!\left[\sum_{k} -\log p_\theta\bigl(x_0^{\sigma(k)} \mid x_0^{\sigma(\lt k)}\bigr)\right].$$

Training is cross-entropy on masked positions — no time conditioning, no Bregman.

DAPD

Dependency-aware parallel decoding via attention  ·  ICML 2026

DAPD

Why Parallel Decoding?

One forward pass returns every marginal $p_\theta(x_i \mid x_S)$ at once — across all masked positions.

The [M] [M] is blue
single inference  →  $p_\theta(x_2\mid x_S),\; p_\theta(x_3\mid x_S)$
  • Unmasking one token per step wastes the other marginals.
  • Unmasking many per step cuts NFE — fewer forward passes, faster sampling.

Parallel decoding is the natural dLLM-native mode.

DAPD

Parallel Decoding: A Formal Problem

The dLLM exposes marginals $p_\theta(x_i \mid x_S)$ on the masked set $\mathcal{M}$. Unmasking $U \subseteq \mathcal{M}$ by independent draws is exact iff the joint factorizes:

$$p_\theta(x_U \mid x_S) \;=\; \prod_{i\in U} p_\theta(x_i \mid x_S).$$

Otherwise the parallel-decoding error is the total correlation:

$$\mathrm{TC}(x_U \mid x_S) \;=\; D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\Bigl(p_\theta(x_U \mid x_S)\,\Big\|\,\textstyle\prod_{i\in U} p_\theta(x_i \mid x_S)\Bigr) \;\ge\; 0.$$
Kim, Jeon, Jeon, No, "Dependency-Aware Parallel Decoding via Attention for Diffusion LLMs", ICML 2026.
DAPD

The Challenge: Marginals, not the Joint

Per step the model gives marginals $p_\theta(x_i \mid x_S)$, not the joint.

DAPD

Parallel Sampling $=$ Graph Coloring

Edge $(i,j)$ $\Leftrightarrow$ $\mathrm{TC}$ contribution $>\epsilon$. A color class is a conditionally-independent set.

Minimize steps (NFE) $=$ fewest color classes $=$ chromatic number $\chi(G)$.

DAPD

Attention $=$ Dependency Oracle

  • Nodes = masked positions (random variables $x_i$).
  • Edges = dependencies between them — that is the dependency graph.

Use the model's own self-attention as the coupling signal:

$$s_{ij} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(a_{ij} + a_{ji}\bigr).$$

Edge $(i,j)$ when $s_{ij} > \tau_t$ — a tractable surrogate for the total-correlation test.

Training-free. No auxiliary model.

DAPD

Algorithm: Coloring with Lookahead

  1. Read symmetric attention scores $s_{ij}$ between masked positions.
  2. Build the dependency graph: edge $(i,j)$ when $s_{ij} > \tau_t$.
  3. Color high-degree nodes first (Welsh–Powell): hubs get their own color and the remaining graph decouples.
  4. Unmask one color class per step. Recompute attention. Repeat.

Lookahead minimizes total steps, not just the current batch size.

(Not greedy maximum independent set — that minimizes the current step, not the total.)

Rainbow Padding

Rainbow Padding: Curing <eos> Overflow

SFT reuses <eos> as terminator and placeholder → a positional bias:

$$\Pr_\theta(\texttt{eos} \mid \text{position } i) \;\uparrow\; \text{as}\; i \to N.$$

Confidence decoding unmasks <eos> first; bidirectional context propagates it backward → the answer collapses.

responseeospad₁pad₂pad₃pad₁pad₂pad₃pad₁pad₂

Fix: a deterministic cycle of distinct pad tokens breaks the bias. ICLR 2026.

Kim, Jeon, Kim, Jeung, No, "Rainbow Padding: Mitigating Early Termination in Instruction-Tuned Diffusion LLMs", ICLR 2026.

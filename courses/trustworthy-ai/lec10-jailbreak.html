What Safety Training Adds

A chat model learns to refuse some requests. What would it mean to get around that, and how would we count it?

Same Request: Refused, Then Answered

Attacker types prompts only · black-box chat · wants an answer the model refused

(a) add a demanded opening line  ·  (b) encode the request in Base64

Automated Attacks Are a Search Loop

Every trip round the loop spends budget: count the queries.

GCG Optimizes a Suffix Toward an Agreeable Start

04

Scaling the Attack Budget

What happens when the attacker simply tries many times, or packs in many examples?

Many Tries Turn a Small Rate Into a Large One

At least one success in $N$ tries: $1-(1-p)^N$, with $p = 1\%$ per try.

Illustrative, not from a study: assumes independent tries with the same $p$.

Many-Shot Jailbreaking Fills the Context With Fake Dialogues

Attacker writes one long prompt · black-box · needs a long context window

Mechanism: in-context learning of the shown behavior.

Anil et al., "Many-shot Jailbreaking", NeurIPS 2024 — Fig. 1 redrawn as a schematic; dialogue contents redacted.

Harmful Responses Rose With the Number of Shots

left % harmful, Claude 2.0, four categories

right NLL of a harmful reply (log–log)

NLL = negative log-likelihood: lower = harmful reply more likely

Few shots: near 0%.

About 256 shots: 40–73%.

Straight lines on the right: a power law.

Anil et al., "Many-shot Jailbreaking", NeurIPS 2024 — Fig. 2 (left: malicious use cases; right: psychopathy evaluation).

Safety Training Shifted the Curve, Not Its Slope

x shots (log) · y NLL of harm (log)

colour how far RL training had run

More RL: curve higher at few shots.

Slope about unchanged, in tested settings.

A prompt warning cut one setting from 61% to 2%; not tested against adaptive attacks.

Anil et al., NeurIPS 2024 — Fig. 5, middle panel (RL, insults), unmodified · Section 5.4 (Cautionary Warning Defense, deception, 205 shots).

Jailbreaks Are Adversarial Inputs Under a Looser Threat Model

Same game: search for inputs that break a learned rule.

Budget Changes Every Number

  • Many-shot: success grows with shots
  • Best-of-N: success grows with tries
  • A found prompt may not work twice

Ask: one try or any of N? And how large is N?

The Attacker Needs One Success

A defense is only as strong as the adaptive attack it survived.

Reading a Jailbreak Number: Checklist

AskWhy it matters

Takeaways

  • Safety training adds refusal on top of capability
  • In tested models, refusal was shallow and low-dimensional
  • Search, many shots, and retries all raise success
  • Defenses help, but adaptive attacks reopen gaps

Report denominator, budget, model, judge with every rate.
